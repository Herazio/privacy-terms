# Privacy Notice for Personal Restricted Applications

**Last updated:** 19 September 2026

## 1. Scope
This Privacy Notice applies to the personal, non-commercial software applications listed under **Covered Applications** below.
These applications are operated solely for private use by their operator. They are not offered as public services and are not intended for use by third parties.
The applications may use restricted production access provided by third-party services. Such access is limited to accounts and resources owned by, or explicitly linked by, the operator.

## 3. Covered Applications
This document applies to personal, non-commercial restricted applications operated by the Operator that explicitly reference this document in their registration, configuration or user interface.

These applications are used exclusively by the Operator and may access only accounts or resources owned by, or explicitly authorised for, the Operator.

## 4. Personal and Restricted Use
Covered Applications are:

- Operated for personal and non-commercial purposes
- Used only by their operator
- Limited to accounts or resources owned or authorised by the operator
- Not made available to the general public
- Not used to provide services to third parties
- Not used to sell, rent or commercially exploit personal data

“Production” refers to the technical environment required to access real services and data. It does not mean that a Covered Application is publicly available.

## 5. Information Processed
Depending on the Covered Application, the following information may be processed:

- Account names, types and currencies
- Technical account identifiers and identification hashes
- Account balances and historical balance snapshots
- Transactions, including amounts, dates, counterparties and payment descriptions
- Session identifiers, consent status and synchronisation metadata
- API responses required for troubleshooting and data integrity
- Application logs and technical error information
- Configuration metadata required to operate the application

Authentication credentials such as banking passwords, PINs, passcodes and multi-factor authentication codes are not collected or stored by the Covered Applications.

## 6. Sources of Information
Information may be obtained from:

- Financial institutions or other service providers connected by the operator
- Information entered directly by the operator
- Local application and synchronisation processes

Access to financial account information occurs only after the operator completes the relevant authorisation and consent process.

## 7. Purposes of Processing
Information is processed solely to:

- Retrieve information explicitly authorised by the Operator
- Organise, store, synchronise and display that information
- Generate private summaries and analyses
- Maintain data integrity and prevent duplicate records
- Secure, maintain and troubleshoot the Covered Applications

Information is not used for advertising, commercial profiling, marketing, data brokerage or services offered to third parties.

## 8. Legal Basis

Processing is initiated by the operator and relates primarily to the operator’s own personal data and accounts.

Where the General Data Protection Regulation applies, processing is based on:

- The operator’s consent to access and process the relevant information
- The operator’s legitimate interest in securely operating and maintaining the applications
- Compliance with applicable legal or security obligations, where relevant

Consent granted to a financial institution or integration provider may be withdrawn through the relevant institution or provider.

## 9. Local Storage

Covered Applications are designed to store normalised information locally on devices or systems controlled by the operator.

Local storage may include:

- Accounts
- Balance snapshots
- Transactions
- Synchronisation history
- Session metadata
- Raw API responses used for verification or troubleshooting

Unless this notice is updated, financial information is not intentionally stored in a public cloud service.

Public source-code or documentation repositories must not contain financial information, session data, private keys or authentication credentials.

## 10. External Providers

Covered Applications may depend on the following categories of provider:

### Integration providers
Integration services may facilitate authorised access to external accounts and services.

### Financial institutions
Financial institutions can authenticate the operator and determine which account information is made available through their regulated interfaces.

### Code and documentation hosting
Services such as GitHub may host source code or public legal documentation. Financial data and credentials are not intentionally stored in public repositories.
Each external provider processes information under its own privacy notice and terms.

## 11. Disclosure of Information
Information is disclosed only when necessary to operate an authorised integration or comply with a legal obligation.
Financial information is not sold, rented or shared with advertisers, data brokers or unrelated third parties.

## 12. International Transfers
The Covered Applications do not intentionally transfer financial information outside the European Economic Area.
External providers may process limited technical or account information in other jurisdictions under their own legal safeguards and privacy notices.

## 13. Retention
Locally stored information is retained for as long as it remains useful for the operator’s personal purposes.
The operator can remove local information by deleting the relevant databases, session files, application data and backups.
Removing local data does not automatically revoke active consents with a bank or integration provider. Those consents must be revoked separately.
Residual copies may remain in local backups until those backups expire or are deleted.

## 14. Security
Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information, including:

- Read-only integrations where possible
- Separation of sandbox and restricted production credentials
- Local storage of financial information
- Restricted file permissions
- Exclusion of secrets and databases from version control
- State validation during authorisation
- Protection of private keys and configuration files
- Minimal logging of sensitive information

No system can guarantee absolute security. The operator remains responsible for securing the devices and systems on which the applications run.

## 15. Automated Decisions and AI
Covered Applications do not make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.
Any generated categorisation, summary or analysis is informational only and must not be treated as financial, legal, tax or investment advice.

## 16. Cookies and Tracking
The Covered Applications do not intentionally use advertising cookies, third-party analytics or behavioural tracking.
The provider hosting this Privacy Notice may apply its own technical logging or cookies under its privacy policy.

## 17. Data Protection Rights
Where applicable, a data subject may have the right to:

- Access personal data
- Correct inaccurate information
- Request deletion
- Restrict processing
- Object to processing
- Withdraw consent
- Obtain a portable copy of relevant information
- Submit a complaint to a supervisory authority

Because the Covered Applications are personal and locally operated, the operator can generally inspect or delete the stored information directly.

Questions can be sent to **privacy@bryanbrandt.com**.
