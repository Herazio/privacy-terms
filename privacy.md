# Privacy Notice for Personal Restricted Applications

**Last updated:** 19 September 2026

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Notice applies to personal, non-commercial restricted applications operated by the Operator that explicitly reference this document in their registration, configuration or user interface.

The Covered Applications are operated solely for private use. They are not offered as public services and are not intended for use by third parties.

## 3. Personal and Restricted Use

Covered Applications:

- Are operated for personal and non-commercial purposes
- Are used exclusively by the Operator
- Access only information or resources owned by, or explicitly authorised for, the Operator
- Are not made available to the general public
- Are not used to provide services to third parties
- Do not sell, rent or commercially exploit personal data

“Production” refers only to the technical environment required to access real services or information. It does not mean that a Covered Application is publicly available.

## 4. Information Processed

Depending on the Connected Service, Covered Applications may process:

- Information and records explicitly made available by the Operator
- Service-provided identifiers and metadata
- Historical and status information
- Authorisation and synchronisation metadata
- Technical logs and error information required for security and reliability
- Configuration metadata required to operate an authorised integration

Authentication secrets, passwords, PINs, passcodes and multi-factor authentication codes are not intentionally collected or stored by the Covered Applications.

## 5. Sources of Information

Information may be obtained from:

- Services explicitly connected and authorised by the Operator
- Authorised integration providers
- Information entered directly by the Operator
- Local application processes

Access occurs only after the Operator completes any authorisation or consent process required by the Connected Service.

## 6. Purposes of Processing

Information is processed solely to:

- Retrieve information explicitly authorised by the Operator
- Organise, store, synchronise and display that information
- Generate private summaries and analyses
- Maintain data integrity and prevent duplicate records
- Secure, maintain and troubleshoot the Covered Applications

Information is not used for advertising, commercial profiling, marketing, data brokerage or services offered to third parties.

## 7. Legal Basis

Processing is initiated by the Operator and relates primarily to the Operator’s own information and resources.

Where the General Data Protection Regulation applies, processing is based on:

- The Operator’s consent to access and process the relevant information
- The Operator’s legitimate interest in securely operating and maintaining the Covered Applications
- Compliance with applicable legal or security obligations, where relevant

Consent granted to a Connected Service or integration provider may be withdrawn through the relevant service or provider.

## 8. Storage and Retention

Information is stored only for as long as it remains useful for the Operator’s personal purposes or is required to operate an authorised integration.

The Operator can remove locally controlled information by deleting the relevant application data and backups.

Removing locally controlled information does not automatically revoke an active external authorisation. External authorisations must be revoked separately through the relevant service or provider.

Residual copies may remain in backups until those backups expire or are deleted.

## 9. External Providers and Disclosure

Covered Applications may use authorised integration, infrastructure, hosting or data-source providers where technically necessary.

Only information required to operate an authorised integration is exchanged with those providers. Each provider processes information under its own terms and privacy notice.

Information is not sold, rented or disclosed to advertisers, data brokers or unrelated third parties. It may be disclosed when necessary to operate an authorised integration or comply with a legal obligation.

## 10. International Transfers

Covered Applications do not intentionally transfer personal information internationally beyond what is required by an authorised provider.

External providers may process limited information in other jurisdictions under their own legal safeguards and privacy notices.

## 11. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect application data, credentials and authorised integrations.

Sensitive information is not intentionally published or included in public source-code repositories. No system can guarantee absolute security, and the Operator remains responsible for securing the devices and systems on which Covered Applications run.

## 12. Automated Decision-Making

Covered Applications do not make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

Any generated summary or analysis is informational only and must not be treated as professional advice.

## 13. Cookies and Tracking

Covered Applications do not intentionally use advertising cookies, third-party analytics or behavioural tracking.

The provider hosting this Privacy Notice may apply its own technical logging or cookies under its privacy policy.

## 14. Data Protection Rights

Where applicable, a data subject may have the right to:

- Access personal data
- Correct inaccurate information
- Request deletion
- Restrict or object to processing
- Withdraw consent
- Obtain a portable copy of relevant information
- Submit a complaint to a supervisory authority

Because Covered Applications are personal and privately operated, the Operator can generally inspect or delete the stored information directly.

Questions can be sent to **privacy@bryanbrandt.com**.
